# Controlled constituent merges — implementation in progress

This workflow is unfinished and is not deployed. `src/crm/merge-profile.ts` currently prepares explicit profile-field decisions only. It does not authorize a merge, persist approval, move records, resolve references or expose a staff merge action.

The approved release now requires completing this workflow before commit/push/deploy. Approval is for any staff member with the required shared-profile, affected-chapter and domain authority; no root-administrator role is required. Internal inventory authorization and draft immutable review/approval/lineage storage are implemented locally and tested, but the durable merge service, privacy-projected staff review, complete domain resolution and staff application remain unfinished. Draft storage is not yet registered as a production migration. The implementation plan records current test receipts.

## Retained records and resolution

Local relational scanning now uses bounded immutable snapshot checkpoints, guarded by request/profile/affiliation/authority revisions and the durable runner's job fence. It includes attachment content metadata/events and retained prior-alias families without rewriting their original record IDs. This internal stage does not seal reviews or enable staff approval. Native Task preservation/projection and the remaining soft-reference inventory must join the final manifest before approval/application can be enabled.

Original financial and activity attribution stays intact. Gift originals/adjustments, giving terms/applications, membership terms/coverage, volunteer submissions, issued documents, native Task links, source identifiers and audit records have retained ownership or history contracts. A merge must use retained identity lineage and current reference resolution rather than update those immutable donor columns or weaken their triggers. Frozen v1 contexts used by existing migrations must stay frozen; changed live views/guards require a new version and additive upgrade.

The impact review must cover both constituents across the organization root, including chapter affiliations and roles/tags; correction requests; household memberships and primary contacts; both relationship directions; gifts and adjustments; pledges/recurring schedules, installments and payment applications; individual/household membership billing and covered members; opportunities, stage history and explicit financial links; volunteer profiles, persistent outreach preferences, invitations/responses, participation and submitted/approved/rejected hours; notes; correspondence snapshots/files/outbox; private attachments; native Tasks/subtasks/completion history; source identifiers; and prior aliases/merge history. Historical documents and audit snapshots remain historical, including their original names and amounts.

Canonical grouping must retain each underlying record's chapter visibility. Combining identity must not grant access to an otherwise hidden affiliation, gift, note, document, Task or hour. Ordinary Tasks still require their native audience plus workspace and relevant CRM permissions. Revoking an original chapter affiliation must continue to hide its private records after a merge. Donor/volunteer overlap is one identity; volunteer discovery must not acquire private hours or notes through merge resolution.

## Review and application still required

1. Authorize both identities and every affected chapter/domain before exposing impact details. Reject unrelated roots. Explain missing authority without leaking inaccessible records or counts.
2. Show explicit survivor and profile-field decisions, chapter role/tag decisions, household/relationship conflicts and the complete affected-record summary. Preserve persistent do-not-contact preferences. Selecting an empty methods/address list is an explicit retained-value decision, never an inferred blank-cell clear.
3. Persist an immutable review/approval snapshot with both profile/affiliation revisions, relevant impact revisions, source mappings and organization authority epoch. Revalidate after edits, new affected records or permission changes; reject stale approvals before any domain application.
4. Apply through the existing leased durable job runner. Retain original identity/profile snapshots and aliases, make interruption/retry safe, and publish identity resolution at a verified atomic boundary. Financial records and totals must not change merely because two identities are grouped.
5. Update every live query, source-identifier lookup, record-access predicate, timeline, correspondence recipient query, report and native Tasks context to resolve lineage consistently while retaining original ownership. Handle subsequent merges and cycles explicitly. Do not enable staff application while some domains still use incompatible reference resolution.
6. Verify complete staff preview/approval/application/reload; large/interrupted/retried merges; archived histories; cross-chapter/root isolation; revoked/stale approvals; exact financial reconciliation; membership/volunteer/opportunity/Task preservation; and repeated additive upgrades. Then include the complete workflow in native browser and release gates.

## Current evidence

Owner-private request creation/reload, immutable profile-review storage and durable preparation-job queueing are now implemented locally as one retry-safe, revision/authority-guarded transaction. Cancellation fences the queued/running preparation and retains history; fresh preparation preserves the cancelled job while creating a new review. Nineteen combined checks pass, including stale profile decisions, concurrent request rollback, cancellation and restart with unchanged donor financial records. The preparation worker, staff projections/interface, approval/publication and complete lineage integrations are not yet implemented or enabled.

The pure profile preparation validates ten explicit field choices, normalizes the resulting profile through the existing contact validator, retains revision expectations and returns a reviewable before/incoming/retained decision list. Four focused cases pass for exact selections/explicit empty addresses, snapshot independence, missing/unknown/inherited choices, opt-out retention, self/cross-root/kind conflicts and invalid revisions. Additional contact regressions and final static verification are recorded in the implementation plan. This is not evidence that controlled merges are delivered.
